# Mechanism: Project Tracing → Outcome: AI Cohesion

This is the clearest framing:

> **Project tracing is the means. AI cohesion is the result.**

## What project tracing means

Project tracing is the continuous source-backed tracking of how work actually evolves:

- what project exists;
- what files/sources define it;
- what decisions were made;
- what changed;
- what is blocked;
- what is stale;
- what tasks/open loops exist;
- what context is reusable;
- what information is valuable enough to preserve;
- what AI agents/tools need to know to act coherently.

This is deeper than task tracking. It traces the operational history and current state of work.

## Why tracing creates AI cohesion

AI tools become incoherent when they lack shared durable context.

Project tracing creates that shared context by maintaining:

- canonical project profiles;
- decision logs;
- source maps;
- open-loop lists;
- stale-assumption checks;
- weekly reports;
- value-scored memory;
- approval boundaries.

Once this layer exists, different AI tools/agents can coordinate around the same ground truth.

Project tracing is the engine that fixes those symptoms.
