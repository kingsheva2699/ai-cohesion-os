# Naming / Positioning Notes

## Strongest positioning

**AI Cohesion OS**

A local-first operating structure that makes AI tools, memory, agents, models, project files, and workflows behave coherently.

## Tagline options

- Make your AI stack act like one system.
- Cohesion for AI-assisted work.
- The missing operating layer for your AI tools.
- Your AI tools, finally working from the same memory.
- Self-maintaining memory and management for AI-heavy workspaces.

## Secondary benefit

Project management is a side effect, not the core sell.

Better wording:

> When your AI stack has shared memory and structure, project tracking becomes automatic.

## Audience

Primary:
- AI-heavy individuals
- founders
- operators
- consultants
- devs using multiple AI tools
- agencies adopting AI internally

Secondary:
- project managers
- knowledge workers
- small teams

## Category language

Use:
- AI cohesion
- AI operations layer
- AI workspace OS
- self-maintaining workspace
- project memory
- execution intelligence

Avoid leading with:
- project management app
- task manager
- productivity app

## Final framing correction

Project tracing is not the final benefit. It is the mechanism.

- **Means:** project tracing / execution tracing / source-backed context tracing.
- **Outcome:** AI cohesion.
- **Side effects:** project management, reporting, tracking, memory, cleaner execution.

Best one-liner:

> AI Cohesion OS traces your projects so your AI tools can share the same memory, decisions, and next actions.
